## 总体评价

This is a comprehensive and well-intentioned review addressing an important question. Your systematic search identified a large body of evidence, and your central finding — that long-term follow-up data are almost universally absent — is important. However, six independent reviewers identified consistent concerns that collectively point to three fundamental issues:

1. **Systematic homogenization**: The review fails to differentiate between qualitatively different types of evidence, gaps, and interventions — ACS (30 years of data) receives the same treatment as LISA (zero data).
2. **Silence blindness**: Key clinical interventions, most notably caffeine therapy, are entirely absent — the CAP trial is one of the few RCTs with actual long-term follow-up data and its omission is significant.
3. **Citation inaccuracy**: Layer 2 verification of 8 key citations found a 0% match rate — effect sizes and confidence intervals systematically deviate from their cited sources.

These three issues compound: the review omits key content, homogenizes what remains, and the numbers used in that homogenization may be inaccurate.

## 致命缺陷（必须修改）

### 1. 引用数字系统性偏差 🔴 NEW（Agent V发现）
8条关键引用Layer 2验证通过率 0%。具体：
- [3] ACS Cochrane: 4个RR全部偏离，数字疑似来自旧版
- [16] LISA Cochrane: Death/BPD RR 0.77→实际0.59
- [5] VTV Cochrane: 通气时间MD −2.36→实际−1.35天
- [20] Ninan 2022: 选择性引用，忽略关键effect modification

**建议**: 逐条核验全部35条引用中的效应量和CI，确保与引用版本一致。

### 2. 方法学身份矛盾 + 无Methods章节
声称"Narrative Review"但使用"systematic search"术语。无独立的Methods章节，检索方法仅5句话。

**建议**: 增加Methods段落(300-500词)，包含数据库、检索日期、检索词、PICO标准、筛选流程。提供简化PRISMA流程图。

### 3. 咖啡因治疗及CAP试验完全缺失
NICU最常用呼吸药物，CAP试验是少数有远期随访的RCT。全文未提及caffeine。

**建议**: 新增咖啡因章节(800词)，纳入CAP试验NEJM 2006 + JAMA 2012 + JAMA Peds 2017。

### 4. 全文均质化/模板化（五层独立收敛）
5位审稿人从不同维度独立发现：ACS(30年数据)=LISA(零数据)=900词。论证结构、篇幅分配、证据呈现均被模板化。

**建议**: 重构5个干预章节——ACS扩展、LISA压缩、章间增加比较性桥梁。让结构本身反映证据梯度。

## 重要缺陷（强烈建议修改）

5. **系统性缺少ARR/NNT**: 30+个RR中仅1个有绝对效应量转换
6. **偏倚评估完全缺失**: 无方法学局限性讨论
