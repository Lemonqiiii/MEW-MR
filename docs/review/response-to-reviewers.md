## Reviewer Comments

### R6-001 — CRITICAL: Table NEUROSIS "5yr NDI signal" Still Incorrect

The clinical decision table (§9.2) still reads "NEUROSIS: mortality + 5yr NDI signal [38, 39]". The corrected NEUROSIS data in §7.1 shows: (a) follow-up was at 18–22 months, not 5 years; (b) NDI did NOT differ significantly (RR 0.93); (c) only mortality was significantly increased (RR 1.37). The table conflates outcomes and uses wrong follow-up duration.

**Resolution**: Changed to "NEUROSIS: increased mortality at 2yr (RR 1.37); NDI not significantly different [38, 39]".

### R6-002 — MINOR: Abstract "emerging" vs Main Text "now published"

Abstract Conclusions uses "emerging school-age data" while §9.2 table and §11 use "now published". SToP-BPD 5.5-year (2025) and NICHD NRN school-age (2026) are published — "emerging" understates current evidence.

**Resolution**: Changed Abstract to "now-published school-age data".

### R6-003 — MUST FIX: PRISMA Figure Missing Caption

§2.5 contains only the embedded PRISMA image with no figure caption. PRISMA 2020 and journal requirements mandate descriptive captions.

**Resolution**: Added figure caption: "Figure 1. PRISMA 2020 flow diagram for the systematic narrative review of postnatal corticosteroids and neurodevelopmental outcomes..."

### R6-004 — MAJOR: 29 References (60%) Missing PMIDs

Per AGENTS.md citation rules, all references must include PMID or DOI. 29 of 48 refs lack PMIDs.

**Resolution**: Added verified PMIDs to all 29 references via PubMed lookup.

### R6-005 — MINOR: "Section 6 and 7" Grammar

§4.3 cross-reference reads "Section 6 and 7" — should be "Sections 6 and 7" (plural).

**Resolution**: Fixed to "Sections 6 and 7".

### R6-006 — MINOR: Conclusions "only now being systematically studied"

§11 phrase conflicts with published school-age data now available. Intentional conservatism vs. draft-era language needs author judgment.

**Resolution**: Refined for accuracy about published vs. unknown evidence.
