# Prematurity and BPD: what general pediatricians should know.
- **PMID**: 36763190 | **Year**: 2023 | **Type**: G | **Importance**: ★★
## Abstract
More and more very low birth weight (VLBW) infants around the world survive nowadays, with consequently larger numbers of children developing prematurity-related morbidities, especially bronchopulmonary dysplasia (BPD). BPD is a multifactorial disease and its rising incidence in recent years means that general pediatricians are much more likely to encounter a child born extremely preterm, possibly with BPD, in their clinical practice.
