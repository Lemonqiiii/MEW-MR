# Lung function in adults born with very low birth weight from young to mid-adulthood.
- **PMID**: 40581701 | **Year**: 2026 | **Type**: E | **Importance**: ★★
## Abstract
<h4>Background</h4>Young adults born preterm with very low birth weight (VLBW; <1500 g), especially those who had neonatal bronchopulmonary dysplasia (BPD), have reduced lung function compared with their term-born peers. We hypothesized that these impairments worsen between young and mid-adulthood.<h4>Methods</h4>We studied two birth cohorts: HeSVA (Finland) and NTNU LBW Life (Norway) with harmonized protocols. Lung function was assessed by spirometry in 115 VLBW (20 with BPD) and 142 controls at mean 36 years.
