## CRITICAL GAPS (Priority ≥7, Absent from Manuscript)

### ❌ Caffeine (I19) — Urgency 7.75
- **Why critical**: 2025 EU Guidelines now recommend prophylactic caffeine for ALL <32wk infants (A1). CAP trial has 11-year cognitive and pulmonary follow-up — arguably the best long-term evidence in neonatology. Caffeine reduces apnea, shortens ventilation, and independently improves neurodevelopmental outcomes.
- **Recommended action**: Add as §8 expansion or new subsection. Minimum: discuss caffeine's indirect respiratory effects (reduced ventilation time → reduced lung injury) and its well-documented neurodevelopmental benefit as context for interpreting other interventions' neurodevelopmental effects.

### ❌ Early Nutrition (I23) — Urgency 7.05
- **Why critical**: 2025 EU Guidelines emphasize starting parenteral nutrition from day 1. Nutrition affects lung development through growth, antioxidant capacity, and immune modulation. Breast milk independently reduces BPD risk.
- **Recommended action**: Note as scope limitation in §10. The interaction between nutrition and respiratory interventions is important but would require a separate review.

### ❌ Magnesium Sulfate (I25) — Urgency 7.20
- **Why critical**: A1-recommended for all women <32wk with impending preterm delivery. Both ACS and MgSO₄ are antenatal neuroprotective interventions — discussing one without the other creates an incomplete picture.
- **Recommended action**: Brief mention in §2 (ACS section) as co-administered antenatal neuroprotective intervention. 2-3 sentences sufficient.
